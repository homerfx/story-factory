# What makes a story go viral on @tylerrstories

(The AI checker reads this file on every deep check. Edit it in plain English whenever your taste changes.)

## The channel
- YouTube Shorts: real stories retold as 25-30 second Roblox animations, narrated in simple American English.
- Audience: kids and teens aged 5-16. A story must be understood instantly, in one listen.

## What the channel's own numbers say (137 Shorts studied, Oct 2026)
Each video was compared with the 30 videos around it, so a 4x video got 4 times the channel's normal views at that time.

**The 32 biggest outliers vs the 22 biggest flops:**
- A kid or teen at the center: **84% of outliers vs 23% of flops**. This is the single biggest factor.
- Set at school or in everyday kid life (a desk, a race, homework, swim class): 25% vs 5%.
- A villain or an unfair adult: 31% vs 14%.
- A bizarre kid body mystery: 19% vs 0%.
- Adult survival or quirky adult trivia: mostly flops.

**Biggest outliers (copy what these have):**
- An 11-year-old girl floated alone in the ocean for 84 hours after the boat captain killed her family (6.4x).
- "They thought she was a kidnapper": the woman watching kids at the pool lost her own daughter there (6.2x).
- A boy saved his whole school by being late (5.5x, 762 comments).
- "Every kid he takes home dies": he only adopts dying kids (4.9x).
- A boy solved the hardest math problem by accident (4.5x, 1,141 comments).
- A 12-year-old was arrested for drawing on her desk (4.4x).
- A girl saved a whole beach because of a school lesson (4.4x).
- Students watched their teacher's rocket explode (4.3x, 1,727 comments).
- Doctors found 526 teeth in her mouth (4.0x).
- 13 kids trapped by their own parents (3.9x).
- A 14-year-old made the most famous dance online and others got the credit (3.9x).
- A 9-year-old got in trouble for winning a race: he accidentally ran the 6-mile race and beat every adult (3.7x).

**Biggest flops (avoid stories like these):**
- A man walked 20 miles to his first day of work (0.19x).
- A man survived 7 lightning strikes (0.21x).
- A whale swallowed a man (0.26x).
- Stuck in the desert 12 days (0.27x).
- A fake necklace turned out to be real (0.29x).
- A woman sprayed Gorilla Glue in her hair (0.30x; already famous everywhere).
- A firefighter / a lifeguard saved a kid (0.33x / 0.42x). The ADULT is the hero, so it flops.
- A teacher set a student's hands on fire (0.41x). Too graphic and painful to watch.

## Why the outliers worked
1. **A kid is the main character.**
2. **The hook is a contradiction**: an ordinary kid thing with an extreme result. Arrested for *drawing*, in trouble for *winning*, saved lives by being *late*, solved it by *accident*, "every kid he takes home *dies*", "they thought she was a *kidnapper*".
3. **Someone misjudges or wrongs the kid** (police, a teacher, a parent, a whole crowd), so viewers feel anger or empathy.
4. **The ending flips the start** in one sentence: he'd beaten every adult; her daughter drowned there; the school is named after him; that's why life rafts are orange.
5. **It makes people argue.** The most-commented videos all have a moral fight: the rocket, the mom who killed her kids, the math problem, the late boy.
6. **It's simple**: one kid, one problem, one payoff. No hard science.

## Rejects / big minuses
- The story only works by describing an ADULT beating, abusing or physically hurting a child → 4 or less. The channel never shows or describes that. (Kids getting hurt in accidents, crashes, survival or medical stories is fine.)
- Anything sexual → 0.
- Staged or fake (skit channels like Dhar Mann, "POV" videos, prank channels, AI videos, scripted "hero" videos) → 2 or less.
- Memes or random funny moments with no story → 3 or less.
- An adult is the main character and there's no strong contradiction hook → 5 or less.
- Politics, race fights, celebrity gossip → 4 or less.
- Already famous everywhere (huge TikTok trend, covered by every story channel) → minus 2.

## Score guide
- **9-10**: kid at the center + a contradiction hook + someone wronged or misjudged + an ending that flips it + a strong emotion. You'd post it today.
- **7-8**: strong, but one part is weaker.
- **5-6**: interesting, but missing the kid, the twist or the payoff.
- **0-4**: doesn't fit the channel.
